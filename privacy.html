Hit The Gap Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Hit The Gap handles information when you use the game. Hit The Gap is designed as a fully offline single-player experience.

1. Information We Collect

Hit The Gap does not collect personal information from players through the app. The game does not require account creation, sign-in, or online submission of gameplay data.

2. Local Device Data

The game stores limited information locally on your device in order to support normal gameplay. This may include:

  • Unlocked levels
  • Best star ratings per level
  • Total stars earned
  • Music on/off preference

This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to the developer by the app.

3. No Analytics, No Tracking, No Ads

Hit The Gap does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or cross-app tracking technology.

4. No Network Features

Hit The Gap is intended to work without an internet connection. The app does not provide leaderboards, multiplayer systems, social posting, or cloud account features.

5. Children’s Privacy

Because the app does not knowingly collect personal information through gameplay, it does not intentionally gather personal data from children.

6. Data Security

Since gameplay progress is stored locally, the primary control over that data remains with the device owner. If the app is removed from the device, locally stored progress may also be removed.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future if the app’s features change. If important privacy-related changes are made, the updated policy should be published with a new effective date.
