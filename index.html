Timing Circle Game

Find the gap. Keep your nerve.

Hit The Gap is a minimalist offline timing game where every tap matters. Launch pins into a rotating circle, read the shrinking safe space, and survive 50 progressively harder levels built for sharp reflexes and clean focus.

See Screens Privacy Policy
Core Features

Fast to learn, strict to master.

The structure stays simple on purpose. Pressure comes from rotation, spacing, speed changes, reversals, and your own timing.

01

One-tap precision

Tap anywhere to fire the next pin and commit to the timing window immediately.

02

50 progressive levels

Each stage increases the challenge through denser layouts, faster spins, and tougher rhythm reads.

03

Star-based replay

Replay unlocked stages to improve your clear quality and earn better star ratings.

04

Offline by design

No account, no ads, and no network requirement. Just launch the game and play.

Screens

Minimal visuals, clear pressure.

Every screen keeps the same visual language: strong contrast, clean geometry, and focused feedback around the circle.
